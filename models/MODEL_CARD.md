# Model card: lung cancer risk screening

Generated by `python -m src.train` on 2026-10-17 (scikit-learn 1.9.0). Do not edit by hand.

## Intended use
Educational demo that sorts survey answers into a lower, uncertain or higher pattern. **Not a diagnosis**, not a
medical device, not validated for clinical use.

## Data
Kaggle "survey lung cancer" CSV: 276 unique respondents after removing duplicates, 238 with
lung cancer and 38 without. About 86% are positive, far above real-world prevalence (roughly 1-2% of
adults), so the output is a **relative score, not a probability of having cancer**. Data provenance and collection
method are undocumented.

## Model
Standardised logistic regression on 14 inputs (GENDER, AGE, SMOKING, YELLOW_FINGERS, ANXIETY, CHRONIC DISEASE, FATIGUE, ALLERGY, WHEEZING, ALCOHOL CONSUMING, COUGHING, SHORTNESS OF BREATH, SWALLOWING DIFFICULTY, CHEST PAIN). Chosen because it
scored best in repeated 5-fold cross-validation and is exactly explainable per prediction.

| Model | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC | Brier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| logistic_regression | 0.933 | 0.989 | 0.07 |
| random_forest | 0.919 | 0.986 | 0.076 |
| gradient_boosting | 0.904 | 0.977 | 0.078 |

Chosen model ROC-AUC 95% bootstrap CI: 0.896 to 0.963.

## Tiers (cut-offs chosen from out-of-fold scores)
Lower below 0.52; higher at or above 0.95; uncertain in between.

| | Lower | Uncertain | Higher |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People with cancer | 5% | 22% | 74% |
| People without cancer | 60% | 32% | 8% |

Only 38 people without cancer exist in the data, so the second row is very noisy.

## Known limitations
- Tiny, imbalanced, unverified dataset; no external validation.
- Symptoms are self-reported and several are common (cough, fatigue, allergy).
- No smoking history detail (pack-years, quit date), the strongest real-world predictor.
- Scores do not transfer to the general population; expect many more false alarms there.
